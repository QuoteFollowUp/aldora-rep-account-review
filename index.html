Aluminum & Glass Products · Sales

Rep Account Review

Upload a rep's sales pivot and call log. Get trends by region and a talking plan for every account. Files stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Version 2026-10-08c

1. Sales by customer

Any of these: the ERP Sales by Customer report (load this year's and last year's period together to compare), an account growth table (Customer, YTD 2025, YTD 2026), or a monthly pivot with a Route column. You can select more than one file.

2. Call log (optional)

Call Log app export (.xlsx). Uses Date, Customer, Contact Name, Barrier to Ordering, Biggest Product Opportunity, Notes / Market Pulse, and Needs Immediate Attention.

3. Quotes by customer (optional)

The combined quotes report (.xlsx). Reads the Customer Summary sheet: Total Quoted, the month columns, and the product $ columns. Used for quoted $ per account, product mix, and close %.

4. Settings

Rep's account list (optional)
Paste the rep's customer names, one per line (copy a column from Excel). The review will only include these accounts. Use this with a company-wide Sales by Customer report.
